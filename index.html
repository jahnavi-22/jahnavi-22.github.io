I'm a Software Development Engineer at Amber, on the team building our AI customer-support system. I like working on things that have to hold up in production, not just in a demo.

Most of my time goes into making that support AI genuinely useful — I re-architected our orchestration layer to cut LLM calls by around 60%, built the search-and-recommendation tooling that surfaces the right options when there's no exact match, and spent a good while getting response times down. I also build partner-facing product features, so I move between deep AI work and full-stack work depending on what the week needs.

Before Amber, I interned at Cleartrip (Flipkart) on their flight search and booking systems in Java and Spring Boot. That's where I learned what production actually feels like — I fixed a pricing bug that was quietly costing real money, and did my share of on-call firefighting on high-traffic systems.

I studied Computer Science at Chitkara University. Outside of work I'm usually building something — most recently a resume-ranker I rebuilt around multi-agent pipelines and evals — or digging into system design. I like problems where solid engineering and a genuinely good user experience have to meet.
